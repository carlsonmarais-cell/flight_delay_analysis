
# Airline Delay Exploratory Analysis and Modeling

Analysis of U.S. airline delay data from the Bureau of Transportation Statistics (BTS), focusing on American (AA), Delta (DL), and United (UA).

The goal is to compare airline performance, understand what drives delays, and identify trends in reliability.

## Tools

### SQL - (old verison)
- Data cleaning and exploration
- Aggregations and summary tables

### Excel
- PivotTables, PivotCharts
- SUMIFS
- XLOOKUP
- Conditional Formatting
- Interactive dashboards and charts
- ANOVA

## Currently Working On

- Regression analysis to identify the strongest predictors of arrival delays
- Forecasting future delay performance
- Estimating impact of reducing carrier delays
- Looking into the effect of the Delta-Northwest merger on delays from 2008-2010

## Findings So Far

- Delta has the lowest average delay minutes per arrival flight among AA, DL, and UA.
- United's average delay duration for delayed flights has improved since 2020.
- Carrier-related delays are similar across the three major airlines, averaging about one hour per affected flight.
